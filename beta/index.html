Thanks for testing Tyre Hunt

Tyre Hunt is a car-spotting game. You photograph real cars and bikes in the wild, a judge verifies each shot on the spot, and every verified capture fills your Registry. This page has the downloads, what to try, and the form that reaches the person building it.

Five things to try
  1. Capture a car. Point the camera at any real car or bike and press the shutter. The judge names it, grades the rarity and your shot.
  2. Claim your tag. Tap the ring at the top left, pick a handle, then add your email so your Registry follows you to any phone. No password.
  3. Chase the Car of the Day. One car a day on the Hunts tab, and a bike too if you hunt both. Capture it that day for the stamp; tomorrow brings a new one.
  4. Look at the Feed. See what other hunters found, like a shot, and check the Car of the Day board.
  5. Try something unusual. A truck, a semi, a camper, a tractor, a car with a for-sale sign, a shot at night. Tell us what the judge got wrong.
Good to know
Photos must be your own, taken in the wild. Screens, posters and toy cars are rejected. Free hunters get 30 verified captures a day. Number plates are left as shot, the same as every car site. Your location is only used for your own map and is never shown to other hunters.
