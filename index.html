The loop

Four seconds from windshield to Registry.

No forms, no typing, no guessing what you're looking at. The whole game is one tap and the thing that happens after it.

01

Spot it

Something interesting rolls past or sits under a tarp. Open the app — it lands straight on the camera.

02

Shoot it

One tap. The judge identifies make, model and era, rates rarity, and grades your framing with a note on how to shoot it better.

03

File it

The verdict lands with a chime and the car enters your Registry — numbered, dated, yours. Miss the ID? Correct it in the dossier.
